About Rice Bowl

Two Cuisines. A Diverse Menu.

Rice Bowl is a Houston restaurant offering a selection of Chinese-American and Indonesian dishes.

Its two-menu concept gives customers the opportunity to explore familiar Chinese-American options alongside dishes rooted in Indonesian culinary traditions.

Whether you are looking for fried rice, noodles, chicken, seafood, or traditional Indonesian favorites, the menu offers different options to explore.
